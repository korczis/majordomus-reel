Rendered from code.

Narration, every frame, the music and the sound effects are rendered from code; nothing is filmed, licensed or generated by a video model. The same scenes are composed for each frame: the phone stacks them, the tablet sets the words beside the visual, and the desktop adds the narration as it is spoken and the use cases each scene shows.
