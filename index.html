Majordomus · the reel

Your AI agent says the work is done. Make it prove it.

A vertical film about Majordomus, the supervisory layer for AI coding agents. Narration, captions, every frame, the music and the sound effects are rendered from code. Nothing is filmed, licensed or generated by a video model.

The short cut

The same film in 2:42, for a phone held upright.

Rendered from code

Voice

Edge neural TTS with word boundaries. Every element appears on the word it belongs to, so the timing is derived, never typed.

Frames

Each scene is a section of one HTML page, painted frame by frame in headless Chrome and encoded with ffmpeg.

Sound

Music and effects are synthesised sample by sample from a seed. The reel carries no third-party audio.

Render it yourself

git clone https://github.com/korczis/majordomus-reel
cd majordomus-reel
./make-reel.sh            # needs node, ffmpeg, uvx and Google Chrome
