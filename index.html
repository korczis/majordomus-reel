Sessions and handover

Story

  • Keep the prompts that started the work, below the model rather than around it
  • Hand work over with a question still open, and keep it blocking8:51

  • Hand unfinished work to the next session2:47
  • Record a decision so the next worker can find it8:59
  • Have the session opened and closed without anybody remembering to
  • Record what one worker did in one sitting9:17

  • Close an episode whose client never came back9:17
